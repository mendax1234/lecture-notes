# Summary

The following table gives a quick recap on the amplifiers that we've learned so far.

| Amplifier           | Load / Type | Gain (Av)                                                                                                              | Rout                                                                                         | $Rin$                              | Remark                                                                                   |
| ------------------- | ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Common Source**   | $$R_D$$     | $$-g_m (r_o \parallel R_D)$$                                                                                           | $$r_o \parallel R_D$$                                                                        | $$\infty$$                         | **Inverting**; trade-off between gain and DC headroom.                                   |
| **Common Source**   | CSL         | $$-g_{m1} (r_{o1} \parallel r_{o2})$$                                                                                  | $$r_{o1} \parallel r_{o2}$$                                                                  | $$\infty$$                         | High gain without large DC voltage drop; suffers from Miller effect.                     |
| **Source Follower** | —           | $$\approx \frac{g_m}{g_m + g_{mb} + 1/r_o} \approx 1$$                                                                 | $$\frac{1}{g_m + g_{mb}} \parallel r_o \approx \frac{1}{g_m}$$                               | $$\infty$$                         | Non-inverting voltage buffer; gain $$< 1$$; low output impedance.                        |
| **Common Gate**     | $$R_D$$     | $$\approx (g_m + g_{mb}) (r_o \parallel R_D)$$                                                                         | $$\approx R_D$$                                                                              | $$\approx \frac{1}{g_m + g_{mb}}$$ | Non-inverting; low $$R_{\text{in}}$$; no Miller effect (good high-frequency response).   |
| **Common Gate**     | CSL         | $$\approx (g_m + g_{mb}) (r_{o1} \parallel r_{o2})$$                                                                   | $$\approx r_{o1}\parallel r_{o2}$$                                                           | $$\approx \frac{1}{g_m + g_{mb}}$$ | Current buffer; non-inverting; limited by load resistance.                               |
| **Cascode**         | CSL         | $$\approx -g_{m1} [r_{o3} \parallel (g_{m2} r_{o2} r_{o1})] \approx -g_{m1} r_{o3}$$                                   | $$\approx r_{o3}$$                                                                           | $$\infty$$                         | Gain is bottlenecked by the simple current source load ($$r_{o3}$$).                     |
| **Cascode**         | Cascode CSL | $$\approx -g_{m1}(r_o' \parallel r_o'') \approx -g_{m1}[(g_{m2} + g_{mb2})r_{o1}r_{o2} \parallel g_{m3}r_{o4}r_{o3}]$$ | $$r_o' \parallel r_o'' \approx (g_{m2} + g_{mb2})r_{o1}r_{o2} \parallel g_{m3}r_{o4}r_{o3}$$ | $$\infty$$                         | Extremely high intrinsic gain; shields Miller capacitance; reduced output voltage swing. |
