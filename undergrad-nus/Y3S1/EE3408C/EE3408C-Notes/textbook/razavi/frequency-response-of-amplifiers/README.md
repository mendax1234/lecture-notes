# Frequency Response of Amplifiers

So far, our amplifier analysis has focused on **low-frequency behavior**, where the effects of circuit capacitances can be neglected. In practical analog circuits, however, **parasitic capacitances become significant at higher frequencies**, limiting the amplifier's **bandwidth and thus the gain**.

In this chapter, we will first review the key concepts of **frequency response**, then study the frequency response of several amplifiers we have learned, including the **CS amplifier, source follower, CG amplifier, and cascode amplifier**.
